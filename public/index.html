      Rules

      How to play

      1. On your turn, tap the gap between two neighbouring dots to draw a line.
      2. If your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours and you go again.
      3. If you don't close a box, the turn passes to the next player.
      4. When every box is taken, the player with the most points wins. Ties share the win.
      5. Star boxes (if the host turns them on) are worth 2 points.
      6. Each turn has a timer. Run out of time and your turn is skipped.
      7. Power-ups (on by default): everyone gets three, each usable once per game on your own turn — Freeze makes the next player miss a turn, Double lets your next line keep your turn, Bomb erases any line that isn't part of a claimed box.

      Tip Avoid drawing the third side of a box — the next player will simply close it.
